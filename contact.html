Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common student and parent inquiries.

Parents and visitors can meet the Principal from Monday to Thursday between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM with a prior appointment from the principal's office. For teacher meetings, Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) are organized at the end of each academic term.

Admissions for Intermediate (1st Year / HSSC-I) open immediately following the declaration of Matriculation results by the FBISE and regional boards. Prospectus and admission forms are available at the college admissions window or online via the Federal Directorate of Education portal.

Alumni requiring Character Certificates, College Leaving Certificates (CLC), or FBISE Migration NOCs can submit a written application to the College Admin Branch along with a copy of their result card and CNIC/B-Form. Processing typically takes 2 to 3 working days.
