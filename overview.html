OFFICIAL INSTITUTIONAL PROFILE

Institutional Overview

A comprehensive journey through our heritage, academic excellence, modern infrastructure, and vibrant student community.

Islamabad Model College for Boys (IMCB) G-10/4

stands as one of the premier public educational institutions in the Islamabad Capital Territory. Since its inception, it has developed into a prominent center of learning, catering to thousands of students and offering a holistic academic and extracurricular environment.

Est. 1987 FBISE Affiliated (#1164) FDE Federal Administration ECE to Intermediate
Section 01

1. Heritage and Institutional Standing

Established in 1987 initially as an English medium school, IMCB G-10/4 has progressively expanded its academic scope over the decades. Today, it functions under the administrative control of the Federal Directorate of Education (FDE) and is affiliated with the Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE). The college is widely recognized in the region for its commitment to producing future leaders, professionals, and responsible citizens.

Main Academic Block - IMCB G-10/4, Islamabad
Section 02

2. Comprehensive Academic Programs

The college provides a seamless educational journey, accommodating students from Early Childhood Education (ECE) up to the Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC/Intermediate) level. At the intermediate level, the college offers diverse disciplines supervised by qualified and experienced faculty members. These specialized groups include:

Biology, Physics, Chemistry
Engineering Track
F.Sc Pre-Engineering

Section 03

3. Campus Infrastructure and Facilities

To support its rigorous academic framework, IMCB G-10/4 features a well-developed campus equipped with modern infrastructure. Key facilities include:

Academic Resources

State-of-the-art science laboratories for practical experiments, a dedicated computer lab, and a well-stocked library to support research and self-study.

Support Services

An on-campus infirmary for medical first-aid, a hostel to accommodate students from outside Islamabad, and an organized transport/bus service for secure daily commuting.

Recreational Areas

Designated play areas and sports grounds to foster physical fitness.

Campus Life & Ground Activities
Section 04

4. Student Life and Extracurriculars

Education at IMCB G-10/4 goes beyond textbooks. The institution strives to develop the "mind, body, and spirit" of its students.

Events & Student Leadership

The college features an active Student Council, which empowers boys to take the lead in organizing annual festivals, academic competitions, sports galas, and stage events.

Sports & Athletics

The college places a strong emphasis on athletics, featuring active sports teams (including a competitive cricket team) and regular inter-collegiate tournaments.

Publications & Culture

A dedicated magazine fund supports student publications, allowing boys to express their literary and creative talents.

Section 05

5. Admissions and Financial Structure

As a Federal Government institution, IMCB G-10/4 is known for providing highly affordable, quality education.

Merit-Based Process
Admissions

The admission process is highly structured and competitive, managed primarily through the centralized FDE School Admission Portal. Student intake is based on entrance exams and strictly followed merit lists.

Centralized FDE Portal & Merit Lists
Highly Subsidized
Fee Structure

The fee system is highly subsidized. While exact figures update annually, the structure generally includes a one-time admission fee alongside very nominal monthly tuition fees. Students also contribute small amounts to specific funds that directly improve their campus experience.

Nominal Tuition Fees & Campus Funds
Section 06

6. Strict Discipline and Uniform

IMCB G-10/4 is highly regarded for its disciplined environment. As a "Model College," it strives to maintain a structured atmosphere similar to that of cadet colleges.

Daily Routine
Morning Assembly

Every school day begins with a mandatory morning assembly featuring recitations, the national anthem, and important announcements from the principal.

Recitations, National Anthem & Official Announcements
Model Standards
Dress Code & Grooming

Students must follow a strict uniform policy (typically a white shirt, specific colored trousers, and a blazer or sweater in winter). Grooming standards, such as neat haircuts, are closely monitored.

Prescribed Uniform & Monitored Grooming
Section 07

7. Highly Qualified Faculty

One of the core strengths of the institution is its exceptional teaching staff.

Merit Induction
Experienced Educators

Most professors and teachers are selected on strict merit through the Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC), ensuring they are highly qualified and experienced in their fields.

FPSC Selected & Subject Specialists
Academic Rigor
Board Examination Focus

The faculty places a strong emphasis on preparing students for the Federal Board (FBISE) exams through regular assessments, mock exams, and rigorous testing sessions.

FBISE Focused & Regular Mock Assessments
Section 08

8. Sports and Co-Curricular Culture

The college focuses heavily on the physical and mental grooming of its students alongside regular academics.

Annual Sports Gala

The college organizes an annual sports week featuring highly competitive inter-house and inter-class matches in cricket, football, basketball, and track athletics.

Literary Societies

English and Urdu debates, Qiraat, and Naat competitions are regularly held to build students' public speaking skills and self-confidence.

Academic Exhibitions

Science fairs and general knowledge quiz competitions are active parts of the yearly calendar, encouraging practical learning and curiosity.

Vibrant Student Body
Diverse Environment

The college attracts students from all sectors of Islamabad and the surrounding regions. The availability of a campus hostel also brings in students from other cities, creating a vibrant, diverse, and competitive environment.

Multi-City Intake & Residential Hostel
Proud Heritage
Alumni Motivation

Graduates of the college have built an impressive legacy, going on to become top doctors, engineers, and civil servants, which serves as a great source of motivation for current students.
