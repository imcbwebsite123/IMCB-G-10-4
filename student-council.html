Structure & Governance

Council Wings & Portfolios

Each wing plays an essential role in keeping the college vibrant, disciplined, and academically driven.

Executive Cabinet

Led by the Head Boy and Deputy Head Boy, representing student interests to college leadership and presiding over prefect meetings.

  • Head Boy & Senior Prefect
  • Deputy Head Boy
  • General Secretary
Discipline & Proctors

Assisting teachers in maintaining campus decorum, morning assembly alignment, uniform compliance, and peaceful hallway transit.

  • Chief Proctor
  • Assembly Coordinators
  • Floor & Gate Monitors
Sports & Athletics Board

Organizing inter-house sporting galas, cricket tournaments, football leagues, and promoting fitness among all classes.

  • College Sports Captain
  • Cricket & Football Leads
  • Annual Sports Day Committee
Literary & Debating Society

Conducting bilingual debate competitions (English & Urdu), Qirat & Naat contests, and general knowledge quiz championships.

  • Urdu & English Debating Presidents
  • Annual Naat & Qirat Conveners
  • Quiz Bowl Coordinators
Science & IT Society

Encouraging technical innovation, coding clubs, science exhibitions, and managing computer lab peer assistance.

  • IT & Coding Incharge
  • Science Fair Organizers
  • Media & AV Technicians
Magazine & Editorial Board

Assisting in publishing the college annual magazine, documenting literary submissions, and preserving college memories.

  • Student Editorial Committee
  • Photojournalism & Archive
  • Creative Writing Contributors
